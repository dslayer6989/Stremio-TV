  `NationalGeographic.us@HDEast` at `198.58.104.90:8989/natgeo/index.m3u8`.

  absent from all six inspected stream-health reports.

  explicit alias map. Do not globally merge suffixes or alter affiliate IDs.
- This admits a candidate to ordinary safety, identity, burn-in and full QA. It
  neither makes it a Primary nor certifies current programme identity. The local
  decoder probe failed, so it is **not a qualified backup yet**.
- Its provider host is already used elsewhere in the addon, but differs from
  NatGeo's current Primary. Operational independence remains unestablished.
